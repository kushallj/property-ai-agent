      How the agent works

      A set of small specialist agents, coordinated by a main agent. Anything that commits your team, like a site visit, waits for a person to approve it.

      One conversation, step by step

      channelsLead arrives

      WhatsApp, portal enquiry, Meta lead form, website chat or missed call.

      main_agentUnderstands and routes

      Reads English, Hindi and Hinglish. Asks one question at a time.

      search_criteria_agentCaptures the requirement

      City, locality, BHK, budget in lakhs or crores, timeline, loan status.

      query_databaseMatches inventory

      Searches your listings sheet or CRM and sends the best 3 with price, carpet area and RERA ID.

      lead_scorerScores the lead

      Timeline, budget fit, financing, intent, contact details and engagement, out of 100.

      appointment_agentBooks the visit, you approve

      Checks your calendar, holds a slot, waits for your one-tap approval, then confirms and reminds.
